Our Story

We believe photos belong in your hands, not buried in your camera roll.

Founded by Akash Rajanna, One More Shot was born out of a simple love for real, tangible memories. In a world of thousands of forgotten phone pictures, a printed Polaroid is something you hold, pin to your wall, tuck into your wallet, or gift to someone you care about.

Whether it’s a sun-drenched evening with friends, a college festival, a birthday milestone, or a spontaneous smile, our boutique studio prints each Polaroid with archival photo lab gloss, calibrated margins, and your heartfelt handwritten message.
